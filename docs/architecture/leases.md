# Lease lifecycle and capacity accounting

Reviewer note for Goal 08. This is not an audit. Public launch is disabled. There is no token, fee denomination, or staking module. `chain/x/leases/README.md` holds the full check orders, store layout and indexes, events, invariants, deviations from the spec machine, and the keeper-level tests. This page covers the app wiring, the cross-module flows, and the app-level and devnet tests.

A lease is a committed storage obligation between a scheduler (an `ADMITTED_SERVICE` account), one provider, and one registered content package. `ACTIVE` and `RENEWED` mean that the obligation is committed and holds a capacity reservation. They do not mean that the package is retrievable, that two providers are independent operators, that a provider is in a region, or that the content is published. Observed reachability (`AT_RISK`) stays off chain; no message, field, or state of x/leases records it (REQ-BOUND-004).

## Wiring

`app.New` builds the keepers in this order:

1. An `x/authority` keeper. Its read surface (`Role`, `GovernedLimit`, `DevnetRole`, `DevnetNumeric`, `PolicyVersion`) is what the registries and x/leases consume.
2. The `x/providers` keeper with that read surface and one shared `msgkit.Peers` set.
3. A copy of the `x/content` keeper without the withdrawal hook. x/leases reads content only through it (`CheckOfferable`, `CheckNotWithdrawn`).
4. The `x/leases` keeper: `Roles`, `Limits`, `Params` (the DEC-005 devnet number), and `Policy` are the authority read keeper; `Providers` is the providers keeper; `Content` is the hook-less content copy; `Evidence` is the x/evidence keeper since Goal 09, so a renewal that names an `evidence_id` reads the committed record and is always rejected (`ERROR_CODE_NOT_FOUND`, `ERROR_CODE_FORBIDDEN`, or `ERROR_CODE_EXPIRED`; [evidence.md](evidence.md)). x/evidence itself reads leases through a second copy of this keeper with no evidence reader; both copies share the leases store key.
5. The `x/content` keeper with `Hook` set to the leases keeper. Both content keepers share the content store key. This is the copy given to the content module, the message router, and `authority.WithRegistry`, so a `WithdrawContent` message and a council force-withdraw both void the content's open offers inside the withdrawal transaction.
6. The authority keeper used by the app: `WithRegistry(providers, content).WithPeers(peers)`.
7. `peers` lists `authority`, `content`, `leases`, and `providers` (and `evidence` since Goal 09). A business id is one namespace across those modules (`TestLeaseRetryScenarios` checks lease messages that reuse a content, provider, or authority business id).

`leases.AppModule.RegisterServices` registers `svn.leases.v1.Msg` on the message router and `svn.leases.v1.Query` on the gRPC query router. The closed lease stubs that returned `ERROR_CODE_FORBIDDEN` since Goal 05 are removed; the evidence stub was removed in Goal 09, when x/evidence registered `svn.evidence.v1.Msg` ([evidence.md](evidence.md)). x/leases has a BeginBlock (`ExpireDue`) and no EndBlock, and returns no validator updates: x/authority stays the only validator-set writer. The BeginBlock order is the genesis order (`app.InitOrder`): leases run after providers and content and before authority.

The leases store holds its layout version (consensus version 2) at key `0x00` from InitGenesis on. Version 1 was the goal-05/06 scaffold slot (the module genesis JSON at key `[0x01]`, which lies inside the offer prefix). `app.New` refuses committed state whose leases store has no layout key or another version, as it does for providers and content (`TestAppRefusesScaffoldLayout`); no migration from version 1 is registered. A goal-07 home must be initialized again. No deployed chain exists.

## Who may write

| Action | Outer signer | Inner signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `OfferLease` | `ADMITTED_SERVICE` account (stored as the offer's `scheduler`; an identical replay by another service is `ERROR_CODE_FORBIDDEN`, D-11) | None |
| `AcceptLease` | A `BROADCASTER` account, or the provider's own `ADMITTED_PROVIDER` account (an identical replay by another provider's account is `ERROR_CODE_FORBIDDEN`, D-13) | Provider acceptance, domain `SVN-PROVIDER-ACCEPT-1`, by the provider's current registered key, over the stored offer terms and the lease id |
| `RenewLease` | A `BROADCASTER` account, or the provider's own account | Domain `SVN-PROVIDER-RENEW-1`, current provider key |
| `EndLease` (provider) | A `BROADCASTER` account, or the provider's own account | Domain `SVN-PROVIDER-END-1`, current provider key, over the lease id, the provider id, and the lease's current term version (an end signed before a renewal is `ERROR_CODE_UNAUTHORIZED` after it, D-12) |
| `EndLease` (`authority_end`) | The `AUTHORITY` account | None (a signature is `ERROR_CODE_UNKNOWN_FIELD`); outside DEC-009 |

A council seat key is not a `BROADCASTER`. It has no submitter row, and the ante refuses every lease message it signs with `ERROR_CODE_FORBIDDEN`, in CheckTx and in FinalizeBlock, before any lease handler runs and before its sequence moves (owner ruling DR-06-06; `TestSeatKeysSignOnlyCouncilMessages` in `chain/app`, and the `seat-relay-refused` phase of `TestDevnetLeaseLifecycle`).

A service credential never produces provider consent. The service's ed25519 hosting-intent key presented as the provider acceptance, under the provider's key id or its own, is `ERROR_CODE_UNAUTHORIZED`; the service account relaying the provider's valid acceptance is `ERROR_CODE_FORBIDDEN`; a retired provider key is `ERROR_CODE_UNAUTHORIZED`; a signature in another known domain is `ERROR_CODE_WRONG_DOMAIN` (`TestServiceKeyCannotSignAcceptanceInApp`, REQ-CHAIN-012). The sign payloads are interim (decision request DR-08-04); `chain/x/leases/testdata/sign-vectors.json` holds golden vectors.

Offers and renewals need the DEC-005 devnet test number (3600 seconds in the devnet genesis). It is a devnet test value, not a policy: DEC-005 leaves lease durations and allowances unset. The number is read only under a `shortvideo-dev-` chain id. Without it every offer is `ERROR_CODE_DECISION_BLOCKED`; the app's own `OfferLease` handler run on devnet state under a non-devnet chain id is `ERROR_CODE_DECISION_BLOCKED` while the same message under the devnet chain id succeeds; and a non-devnet genesis cannot carry devnet test parameters at all (`TestDevnetTermDecisionBlocked`). REQ-CAP-008 (sponsor and budget) stays BLOCKED on DEC-005.

## Cross-module flows

- Offer: x/leases checks the message, then `content.CheckOfferable` (content `PENDING`, same version, manifest digest, and package bytes, else `NOT_FOUND`, `WITHDRAWN`, `CONFLICT`, `INTEGRITY`), `providers.CheckNewObligation` (`ADMITTED`, else `FORBIDDEN`), the DEC-005 term, the live pair, the provider's open-offer count, `providers.ReserveProbe` (`CAPACITY`, `OVERFLOW`), and the chain-only bounds: offer lifetime (D-5), 256 open offers per content (D-6), and 128 per content from one scheduler (D-10). An offer reserves nothing.
- Accept: the offer lookup, `providers.VerifyProviderSignature` over the stored offer terms, the relay rule, the lease id, the offer expiry, `content.CheckNotWithdrawn`, `CheckNewObligation`, the live pair, `ReserveProbe`, and the offer deadline (D-3). Then `providers.Reserve` takes the bytes and the lease records the authority policy version at acceptance (`TestLeaseFlowReplayAndQueries` decodes it).
- Renew: the provider's renewal signature, the relay rule, a held lease, content not withdrawn, `term_version` exactly one more, the same bytes and manifest digest, one DEC-005 term past the current deadline, and a deadline still in the future (D-2). The reservation does not change.
- End: a provider-signed end (relayed) or `authority_end`; a lease whose reservation is not held is `ERROR_CODE_UNDERFLOW`. `providers.Release` returns the bytes exactly once. A provider end binds the lease's term version, so an end signed before a renewal cannot end the renewed lease (D-12).
- Expiry: BeginBlock of every block reads the block header time and closes at most the effective `max_expiry_steps` due items (protocol ceiling 128): held leases by (deadline, id) and open offers by (expiry, id), merged in (time, kind, id) order, leases before offers at equal times. A lease's bytes are released once. The budget is one per block for the whole chain: open offers of any admitted service that expire before a lease's deadline are swept first and can delay its release (DR-08-07). An invariant violation (for example a release that would underflow) returns an error from BeginBlock: FinalizeBlock fails and the block is not committed (`TestExpiryInvariantViolationHalts`; fail closed, DR-08-07). A stored `max_expiry_steps` that is not 8 bytes in 1..ceiling panics in x/authority `GovernedLimit`, so FinalizeBlock panics on every node (`TestDamagedExpiryLimitHalts`, which calls FinalizeBlock directly).
- Withdrawal: `WithdrawContent` or a council force-withdraw calls the hook, which voids every open offer of the content (at most `MaxOpenOffersPerContent` = 256, PROPOSED, DR-08-02; one scheduler holds at most 128 of them, D-10) and emits `leases_offer_closed` with reason `WITHDRAWN` before the content's `ContentWithdrawn`. Leases of withdrawn content keep their reservation until they end or expire; their renewal is `ERROR_CODE_WITHDRAWN`. A voided offer cannot be accepted (`NOT_FOUND`) and no new offer can name the content (`WITHDRAWN`).
- Drain and council disable: new offers and acceptances for that provider are `ERROR_CODE_FORBIDDEN`. Existing leases keep their reservations and can still be renewed (the v0 spec rule, decision request DR-08-01, the successor of DR-07-05); the remedy is `authority_end`, one lease per message.
- Repair handoff: a replica on another provider is a new offer and acceptance for the same content id; the pair index is per provider, so it is allowed while the old provider's lease is held, and each provider's reservation is accounted separately. Two leases on two providers are two replicas; the chain does not claim the providers are independent operators.

The withdrawal transaction voids each open offer of the content (at most 256), so its gas depends on that number. `TestWithdrawalAtCapGasInApp` asserts that at 256 open offers both the `WithdrawContent` message and the council `ExecuteChange` commit below 10,000,000 gas, half the transaction gas cap, and that simulating the same signed bytes first changes no committed state and reports exactly the committed gas (asserted equal for both transactions). A client sizes its gas limit with simulation.

## Devnet tests (`chain/devnet`, four svnd processes)

Label: single-machine four-process devnet (not independent operators). The four validators run on one machine with loopback peers; the test commands run them inside one network namespace. Command: `go test -count=1 -timeout 1200s ./chain/devnet/ -run 'TestDevnetLease' -v` (through the netns wrapper in this workspace). The devnet genesis has a `broadcaster` account (`BROADCASTER`) since Goal 08; its key is generated at init and stays under the home directory.

`TestDevnetLeaseLifecycle` phases:

1. Register and council-admit p1, p2, and a 10 MB provider p3; register contents in one multi-message transaction.
2. Acknowledgement is not commitment: an acceptance broadcast with `broadcast_tx_sync` passes CheckTx (code 0) and fails in its block with `ERROR_CODE_CAPACITY`; p3's reserved bytes are unchanged.
3. Timeout and retry: the client sends an acceptance, does not wait, and re-signs the same business payload at the next sequence (a distinct tx hash). Both commit with code 0: one `LeaseAccepted`, one `leases_result`; one held lease and one reservation. A changed payload under the business id is `REPLAY`.
4. Node down: v4 stops; the broadcast to v4 fails; the same signed bytes commit once through v1; a rebroadcast is refused (CheckTx sequence error, or the mempool cache); v4 restarts, catches up, and answers the same query bytes as v1 at a common height.
5. Renewal and end retries are idempotent; stale renewal `STALE_VERSION`, other bytes `CONFLICT`, end again with a new business id `UNDERFLOW`, a second replica on the same provider `DUPLICATE_ID`, the service key as acceptance `UNAUTHORIZED`, the service as outer signer `FORBIDDEN`; authority end.
6. Expiry by header time: the last height whose header time is before the deadline holds the lease (effective-1) and the first height at or after it expires it (effective), releasing its bytes once; both heights and header times are printed.
7. Bounded drain: a council sets `max_expiry_steps` to 4; 24 leases and 6 offers share one deadline and expiry; the per-block counts from `/block_results` (`mode=BeginBlock`) are `[4 4 4 4 4 4 4 2]` in (time, kind, id) order, and the block before closes none.
8. Withdrawal voids the open offer of the content and keeps the active lease's reservation; accept of the voided offer `NOT_FOUND`, a new offer `WITHDRAWN`, renewal `WITHDRAWN`; council disable of p2: offer and accept `FORBIDDEN`, renewal allowed, authority end releases; repair handoff of c6 to p1.
9. Query bytes are equal on all four nodes at one height; every block is replayed into an in-process application, which must reproduce the header `app_hash` of the next block on all four nodes, keep the full state unchanged at every committed rejection, and answer the same query bytes.
10. Every committed event is parsed (`ParseCommitted` for transaction events, `ParseBlockEvent` for BeginBlock events) and scanned for forbidden names, canaries, and URLs.

`TestDevnetLeaseHaltRestart` (REQ-HOST-007): a lease's deadline is a real-time obligation in the signed terms. The provider signs a renewal and does not broadcast it. All four validators stop before the deadline and restart after it has passed in real time. The last pre-halt height is measured after all four stopped (the highest committed application version in their stores; blocks can still commit while the first validators stop), and its header time, read after the restart, must be before the deadline with the lease held. The first height whose header time is at or after the deadline expires the lease, the height before still holds it, and the offline renewal submitted after the restart is `ERROR_CODE_CONFLICT`: a halted chain does not turn an offline note into a renewal. The reservation is released once and a replay reproduces every node's header `app_hash`. CometBFT block time after a restart comes from the votes of the previous commit, so the first block after the restart can still carry a header time from before the halt; the test finds the effective height by header time, not by assuming it is the first new block.

Measured numbers (gas, heights, counts, hashes) are cited only from the evidence logs of the committed tree (`docs/evidence/goal-08.md`), not from this page.

## Decision requests

Interim choices are reversible and are not owner approval. Details are in `chain/x/leases/README.md`.

- DR-08-01 renewal after drain or council disable (successor of DR-07-05): v0 allows it; the remedy is `authority_end`, one lease per message. A disabled, possibly compromised provider can keep extending its own leases.
- DR-08-02 `MaxOpenOffersPerContent` = 256 (PROPOSED, D-6), bounding the withdrawal hook's work, and `MaxOpenOffersPerContentPerScheduler` = 128 (PROPOSED, D-10). D-10 stops one admitted service from filling a content's 256 D-6 slots by itself; two admitted services together can, indefinitely, by re-offering as slots free. One admitted service alone can still stop every other scheduler from offering any content id: it fills the shared `max_open_offers` slots of every admitted provider and re-offers as slots free (DR-08-13).
- DR-08-03 `MaxOfferLifetimeSeconds` = 86400 (PROPOSED, D-5, not a DEC-005 duration). It bounds one offer's lifetime, not how long a service keeps a shared cap full; it clears a service's offers within a day once the service stops offering.
- DR-08-04 provider ACCEPT, RENEW, and END payloads (field sets, decimal-string integers, lease id bound, no chain id). The END payload binds the lease's current term version (D-12, interim). Without it, an end signature that became public in a rejected transaction, or sits in an outbox, would end the renewed lease against the provider's later intent until a key rotation.
- DR-08-05 real-time deadline guards D-2 and D-3 (stricter than v0).
- DR-08-06 offer ids are never reused (D-4); an export drops the closed-offer markers.
- DR-08-07 expiry, the chain choices around the Gate-A cap of 128 items per block (the cap itself is accepted): BeginBlock placement, (time, kind, id) order, a budget shared by leases and offers (Goal 09 gave evidence no budget: records are not swept, their EXPIRED state is computed at read time, owner ruling on Q4), and halting on an invariant violation. Offers of any admitted service share the budget with held leases and are swept first when they expire earlier: every lease release due at or after their expiry can be late by up to ceil(open offers due / budget) blocks, 8 per admitted provider at the defaults and up to 1,024 per provider at a council budget of 1. Options: due leases before due offers within a block (a deviation from the op_expire order), a separate offer budget, or the spec order (interim).
- DR-08-08 protocol additions: a typed offer-closed event, Lease and Offer by-id queries, a structured Deadlines cursor, an unfiltered ActiveLeases, and sponsor attribution (`Lease.scheduler`, or scheduler and offer id on `LeaseOffered` and `LeaseAccepted`).
- DR-08-09 renewal window: a provider can renew any time while the reservation is held and stack terms.
- DR-08-10 one integer encoding rule for business payloads across modules.
- DR-08-11 an identical `OfferLease` replay by another admitted service is `ERROR_CODE_FORBIDDEN` (D-11), stricter than the spec. Without it, a service that copies a pending offer becomes its scheduler and the original sender reads code 0.
- DR-08-12 `RenewLease`/`EndLease` check the provider signature before the business gate (spec order), so a retry after a key rotation, or an end retried after a renewal (D-12), is `ERROR_CODE_UNAUTHORIZED`, not idempotent.
- DR-08-13 the per-provider open-offer cap (1,024) is shared by all schedulers, as in the v0 spec: one admitted service can keep a provider's slots full indefinitely and block every other scheduler's offers to it, a repair handoff included (`TestOneSchedulerRefillsProviderSlots`).
- DR-08-14 an identical `AcceptLease` replay by another provider's `ADMITTED_PROVIDER` account is `ERROR_CODE_FORBIDDEN` (D-13), stricter than the spec; a `BROADCASTER` replay stays idempotent. Without it, a provider account reads code 0 for an obligation it does not own.

## Handoff

- Goal 09 (done, [evidence.md](evidence.md)): `Deps.Evidence` is wired (`RenewalEvidence`); evidence takes no expiry budget (owner ruling on Q4); x/evidence reads `leases.Keeper.Lease` for the DEC-010 manifest digest comparison and the lease binding. Evidence never extends a lease.
- Goal 11 broadcaster: a broadcast acknowledgement (CheckTx runs only the ante chain) is not success; poll the committed result. Resubmitting the same business id and payload under a new tx hash is idempotent (code 0, `leases_result`) only if every check before the business gate still passes, because each runs again on the resubmission. Changes committed between the attempts can turn an identical resubmission into a rejection: another scheduler (`FORBIDDEN`, D-11), another provider's account (`FORBIDDEN`, D-13), a provider key rotation (`UNAUTHORIZED`, DR-08-12), a renewal before an end retry (`UNAUTHORIZED`, D-12), a council change that lowers `max_package_bytes` below an offer's bytes (`BOUNDS`), or a change of the signer's role. Another payload is `REPLAY`; a rejected transaction is in its block with its svn code. Read the committed result by tx hash or lease state before resubmitting. Size the gas of a withdrawal with many open offers by simulation.
- Goal 12 indexer: the typed lease events plus `leases_offer_closed`; `msgkit.ParseCommitted` for transaction events and `msgkit.ParseBlockEvent` for BeginBlock events; reconcile offers through `Deadlines` and leases through `ActiveLeases` and the events.
- Goal 16 provider agent: the sign-bytes builders and `chain/x/leases/testdata/sign-vectors.json`; an end signs the lease's current term version, so after a renewal the agent signs a new end; deadlines are real-time obligations even while the chain commits no blocks; the agent compares deadlines with its own clock and shows the uncertainty.

## Residual risks

- Raw `/store/leases/*` ABCI queries are not metered or paged (the SDK serves them from the IAVL store); the CometBFT RPC port must not be public. Only gRPC-routed queries run on the query gas meter.
- Lease history grows without limit; there is no per-account lease limit while DEC-005 leaves allowances unset. Gas meters every write.
- One admitted service can keep a provider's 1,024 open-offer slots full indefinitely by re-offering (DR-08-13), and by filling every admitted provider it blocks every other scheduler's offers of any content id; two admitted services can fill a content's 256 slots (DR-08-02). The offer lifetime (D-5) does not limit this.
- Offers of any admitted service share the per-block expiry budget with held leases: lease releases due after earlier-expiring offers can be late by up to ceil(open offers due / budget) blocks, 8 per admitted provider at the defaults (DR-08-07).
- A renewal or end retried after a provider key rotation, or an end retried after a renewal (D-12), is `ERROR_CODE_UNAUTHORIZED` even when the first one committed (DR-08-12).
- The sponsor of an accepted lease is only in the used-offer marker and the `OfferLease` signer; no query or typed event returns it and an export drops it (DR-08-08).
- A disabled provider can keep renewing its leases (DR-08-01).
- An expiry invariant violation halts the chain (fail closed, DR-08-07).
- An export drops business ids and closed-offer markers; app-level re-import is BLOCKED.
- The devnet runs four processes on one machine; it is not an independent-operator network.
