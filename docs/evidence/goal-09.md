## Residual risks

- An admitted auditor can lie, in both directions. The compared manifest digest is public, so `RECORDED` means "the auditor reported the manifest digest", not "the bytes were retrieved". A dishonest auditor can record `REJECTED` against a healthy provider. A `REJECTED` record never expires, and the chain cannot tell whether the provider or the auditor caused it. Nothing is paid or slashed.
- Endpoint source (N2): a PASS from an operator-configured endpoint is signed and `RECORDED` although nothing binds that endpoint to the provider. Even a capability-manifest endpoint shows only that the provider account declared it. The chain cannot see the source, and a dishonest auditor can still sign `REJECTED` for any endpoint.
- A provider can avoid `REJECTED` records from an honest producer by being unreachable, by redirecting, by returning a compressed answer, or by answering a range request with 416 or 501. Those audits are INCOMPLETE and stay off chain, and the missing `RECORDED` record is the signal.
- A `RECORDED` record written while a lease was held keeps reading `RECORDED` after a later `EndLease`, `WithdrawContent` or lease expiry, until its own expiry (at most 86400 s, never past the lease deadline at recording).
- A compromised or dishonest auditor cannot be removed on chain in v0, and its records stay (DR-09-08).
- The chain cannot detect a shared operator or a shared key. A provider and an auditor run by one operator, or a provider that sets its signing key to an auditor's public key, look like independent parties (D-14 withdrawn).
- The auditor account key also signs its attestations (Q1). The domain-separation argument is unreviewed (devnet beta only), and the key cannot be rotated.
- Business-id squatting (DR-09-28): a persistent front-runner, with a motive if it is the audited provider, can delay recording at no fee. Drawn retry ids do not stop the front-running of a broadcast already in the mempool.
- Evidence state grows forever: at most 8,970,240 records per auditor per year, bounded per auditor per epoch, not over time.
- Raw `/store/evidence/*` ABCI queries are unmetered and unpaged, and they show the stored state, not the reported state. The CometBFT RPC port must not be public.
- A historical query reports state at the SDK's query block time. When the node has no commit info for that height, this falls back to the latest header time. Right after a restart that time is zero, and the query fails closed (`ErrNoBlockTime`).
- The reviewer's `--node` source is an unverified ABCI query that trusts the node. `--record` is a local file.
- A sampled check covers only the sampled segments. A provider can serve the requested ranges from another host.
- An epoch boundary between signing and inclusion makes a batch `STALE_VERSION`. A lease or content change during inclusion fails its provider's whole batch, which is atomic.
- Steps 2-10 run before the count check, so a rejected message with many items does unpaid CPU work, up to the transaction size and the 65536-byte payload cap.
- Evidence ids are linkable to the auditor and to the off-chain report by design. Reports hold no personal data. Opaque ids and digests stay linkable by anyone who holds the preimage.
- The producer cannot recognise network-specific NAT64 prefixes. The network policy is PROPOSED (DR-09-10). REQ-SEC-010's default-reject test belongs to Goal 25.
- The N1 guard is a text parser. It catches accidental workflow edits like its mutation cases, but a deliberate edit could get past it, or change the guard itself. With the skip, the CI `go` job no longer runs the devnet tests without `-race`; they run only in the race shards.
- When the round wait hits, the `devnet-evidence` shard has a thin margin under its 15-minute `go test` timeout. The local shard sample is `devnet-evidence` exit 0, 302 s. No CI timing exists (BLOCKED item 4).
- A FAIL item is dropped quietly on a rebuild when its stored report cannot be read or parsed. It is listed in `Dropped`, with no separate warning.
- When a devnet test binary panics on a timeout, its validator processes keep running and its temp directory stays. This harness gap is not fixed.
- The fabricated but self-consistent report case (`TestSelfConsistentFalseReportIsConsistent`) is covered only with fakes, at unit level.
- The devnet is four processes on one machine. Every auditor in every run is one operator on one machine, and node ids do not prove independent replicas.
